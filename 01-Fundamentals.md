**What is Cybersecurity?**
-> Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, mobile devices, servers, networks and data from unauthorised access, attack, damage or theft
Inshort, it is the digital security of the internet from unauthorized access

**CIA traid**
Confidentiality - Only authorized people can access data.
Integrity - Data cannot be altered improperly.
Availability - System and data stay accessible when needed.

**Types of Hackers**
- *White Hat Hackers* - This are good hackers that work with developer and finds vulnerability in the system and fix it so that the bad hackers don't use it to steal credentials and important data.
- *Black Hat Hackers* - This are the bad hacker that break the system to steal credentials and data to sell in black market or generally use for bad purpose.
- *Grey Hat Hackers* - This are the mix of the above 2 usually move between the 2 types they are neither good nor fully bad.

**5 Steps of Hacking**
1. Reconnaissance - To gather information and details about the target before attacking
2. Scanning and Enumeration - Actively interact with the system to discover details
3. Exploitation - Attempt to demostrate a security weakness by obtaining access
4. Maintaining access - After the access is granted, assess what an attacker do next
5. Reporting - To report the vulnerablities
